# Mission conduct: public evidence and limits

**One continuous digital TMC session with 1,501 sequential state records; no campaign of 1,501 independent missions.** The public record distinguishes the native digital session from the legacy offline corpus, and observation within a session from comparative effectiveness.

| Evidence | Denominator | Observation | What it does not establish |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Civilian digital bench: mission continuity ([Mission Lab](https://tmc.stasysos.eu/lab)) | One 1,500-second session, with 1,501 sequential records | The same TMC session retained its assigned objective. A new proposal was not admitted under invalid conditions; a later permitted continuation had an independently observed consequence in the digital model. The viewer checks the count and the trace SHA-256. | Independent mission repetitions, physical flight, mission completion, or an improvement over an incumbent controller. |
| Historical offline advisory corpus | Historically reported: 1,218 archived logs discovered; 1,213 parseable and replayed offline in the legacy advisory evaluation | Coverage reported for a different workflow. A corpus-level independent rerun of the historical baseline has not been established. | A native TMC run on each file, completed missions, a success rate or comparative effectiveness. |

The 1,501 state records are time samples from the same session. They are not 1,501 new intent decisions. Likewise, an offline advisory flag is not a mission outcome. A subset eligible for further analysis does not change that distinction. None of these denominators can be combined.

## Comparative effectiveness: not established

The public evidence demonstrates bounded behavior and a digital consequence in the tested configuration. It does not establish a population success rate, a performance uplift, or qualification for any partner platform. A rating against an incumbent requires a separately agreed civilian evaluation, comparable inputs, independent outcomes and a documented denominator. No such comparative result is claimed here.

The public viewer is a bounded demonstration, not a substitute for independent reproduction. The legacy corpus counts are a historical report, not a new or independently reproduced benchmark; raw records and proprietary artifacts are not distributed in this package.
