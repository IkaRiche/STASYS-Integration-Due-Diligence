# Evidence status, qualification and information access

**The commercial goal is a whole-system integration evaluation.** The currently accepted TMC, DRC, Morphic Vision, Morphic AI and Guardian Veil results come from separate source-owned configurations. They do not constitute a verified single deployed autonomy product. See the [integrated evidence matrix](INTEGRATED_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md), [proposed external contract](EXTERNAL_INTERFACE_CONTRACT.md) and [owner-controlled test protocol](BLACKBOX_EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md).

This is a **controlled public integration overview**, not an independent scientific proof repository, a certified safety manual or a product release.

**Documented scope:** externally described deterministic mission-conduct and runtime-assurance mechanisms have controlled laboratory research. Demonstrated scope depends on its individual test configuration. A requirements visualization is not equivalent to a complete validated onboard mission.

**Qualification to be established separately:** compatibility with a particular vehicle/controller, runtime resource budget, admissible localization and sensor integration, power/reset behavior, independent outcome validation, flight/field safety and applicable regulatory approval.

This distinction is necessary for reliable procurement decisions. It does not authorize or imply claims about operational readiness or certification.

For the numerical scope of the public evidence, see [mission conduct](MISSION_CONDUCT_EVIDENCE.md) and [timing](TIMING_EVIDENCE.md). [Related evidence tracks](RELATED_EVIDENCE_SCOPE.md) are assessed separately. Historical offline replays are not current TMC-run missions; a local compute measurement is not a platform latency measurement.

## Proprietary boundary

Internal computation topology, program structure, source code, algorithmic implementation, experimental datasets, detailed architectural composition and hardware pin-level integration remain outside this public package. Selected owner-operated demonstrations and external acceptance tests may be discussed independently of access to these materials.

## Independent assessment

Reviewers may assess the proposed data contract, formulate challenge inputs and specify observable acceptance criteria. They cannot independently reproduce undisclosed internal experiments from this package alone. A later non-sensitive demonstration or agreed evaluation protocol may address a particular system claim under separate terms.
